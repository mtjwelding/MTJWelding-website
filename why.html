Firefighter-Owned & Operated

Built by a Tradesman.
Trusted by Neighbors.

MTJ Welding & Fabrication is run by a working Salt Lake City firefighter. On duty when the call comes in — in the shop the rest of the time, building metalwork that holds up for decades.

That dual background matters. The fire service runs on preparation, communication, and accountability. Those same values run MTJ. You get a straight answer on scope, a clear quote, and work that looks as good on year ten as it did on day one.

There's no crew of sub-contractors, no middleman markup, and no upsells. One person answers the phone, does the work, and stands behind it.
