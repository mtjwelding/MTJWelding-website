Custom Fabrication

Built-to-spec steel work for residential and commercial projects. Designed, fabricated, and finished in-house.

From front-porch handrails to large commercial driveway gates and dumpster enclosures — MTJ fabricates custom steel work to your exact spec. Every piece is measured, cut, welded, and finished in our SLC shop before it ever hits your jobsite. No off-the-shelf parts dressed up as custom work.

Handrails & Railings

Stair handrails, porch railings, guardrails, and ADA-compliant railing systems for residential and commercial properties.

Driveway & Pedestrian Gates

Swing and slide gates — single or double leaf — fabricated from steel or iron to complement your property's style.

Fences & Enclosures

Security fencing, dumpster enclosures, equipment cages, and decorative iron fencing built to your layout.

LiftMaster Gate Operators

Authorized dealer and installer of LiftMaster swing and sliding gate operator systems.

MTJ is an authorized LiftMaster dealer, which means we sell and install the real thing — not knockoffs or grey-market units. We spec the right operator for your gate, fabricate the gate to match if you need one, and install the full system as a matched pair. One contractor, one install, one point of contact if anything ever needs attention.

Swing Gate Operators

Single and double-leaf swing operators for residential and commercial properties with the clearance to swing.

Sliding Gate Operators

Rack-and-pinion slide operators for tight lots, steep driveways, and high-cycle commercial entries.

Gate + Operator Packages

We fabricate the gate and install the operator as a matched system — balanced, spec'd together, and ready to run.

Access Control

Keypads, remotes, smartphone control, and vehicle loop sensors installed as part of your system.
